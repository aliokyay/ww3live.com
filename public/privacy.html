Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026 | Effective Immediately

Welcome to WW3 Live Radar (WW3LIVE). Your privacy and digital security are critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our interactive Global Threat Map and Conflict Radar platform. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

WW3 Live Radar is designed primarily as an open-access geopolitical intelligence and real-time visualization platform. Users can browse live map feeds, operational summaries, and financial logistics data without registering for an account or submitting personal identification details. However, certain standard technical data is automatically processed when you visit our site:

  • Log Files & Technical Telemetry: Like most standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure automatically collects non-personally identifiable information provided by web browsers and servers. This includes your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, language preference, referring URLs, date and time stamps of visits, and pages viewed. This data is used strictly to maintain server stability, prevent Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks, and analyze aggregate traffic trends.
  • Local Browser Storage: Our application may utilize browser localStorage or session state to remember your interface preferences—such as your selected display language, pinned geographical regions, audio alert mute settings, and map tile styles (e.g., Dark, Satellite, or Topographic view). This information remains stored locally on your device.

2. Cookies, Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

To fund server infrastructure, data feeds, and ongoing development of our free live war tracking platform, WW3 Live Radar utilizes third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

  • Third-Party Vendors & Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (small text files placed on your device) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables Google and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to our users based on their visit to WW3 Live Radar and/or other sites across the web.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

You can also choose to disable or selectively turn off cookies in your individual browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies does not affect your ability to view the live conflict map.

3. Third-Party Services, Widgets & External Links

WW3 Live Radar integrates external data streams and embedded widgets to provide comprehensive situational awareness:

  • Map Tile Providers & Geospatial APIs: Interactive maps are rendered using Leaflet alongside cartographic tiles from CartoDB, Esri ArcGIS, and OpenStreetMap contributors. Aviation telemetry is sourced from public OpenSky Network endpoints.
  • Financial & Commodity Widgets: Real-time market quotes and ticker tapes in our Logistics view are powered by embedded TradingView widgets, which operate under TradingView's own privacy and cookie policies.
  • External News Sources: Incident markers on our radar contain direct outbound links ("Access Source") to third-party journalism outlets and defense publications (such as Al Jazeera, BBC, Defence Blog, Crisis Group, and others). We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including the European Economic Area (under the General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR) and the State of California (under the California Consumer Privacy Act - CCPA), you hold specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access, update, or request deletion of any personal information we may hold about you.
  • The right to object to or restrict the processing of your personal data, including opting out of targeted advertising cookies via our consent management banner or browser controls.
  • The right to know that WW3 Live Radar does not sell personal user contact lists or private identity records to third-party data brokers.

5. Data Security & Children's Privacy

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption and security rules to protect our platform against unauthorized access or alteration. WW3 Live Radar does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. Due to the mature nature of geopolitical conflict reporting, this platform is intended for general adult and professional audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our technical architecture, legal obligations, or advertising partnerships. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the website following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of such changes.
